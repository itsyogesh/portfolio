Chargespot is the EV charging platform we're building at **Charge23 Labs**, the company I co-founded with my wife Evisha in August 2023. It's a DPIIT-recognised startup, incubated at AIC Sangam, and the most hardware-adjacent thing I've ever worked on.

## What we're building

The consumer side is an EV companion app: vehicle setup, battery-aware trip planning, charging discovery, session start and monitoring, and OBD telemetry. Underneath sits the operator side — a charging station management system speaking **OCPP and OCPI (v2.0–2.2.1)**, so any compliant charger can join the network.

## The stack

A Flutter app, a Django backend with a Go station server, TypeScript web dashboards, and Terraform-managed AWS infrastructure — around twenty repositories under the charge23 organisation.

## Honest status

Pre-launch. We raised a small round, built a five-person team, and burned most of it learning how hard EV infrastructure really is. We're still here, still building — the next milestone is a pilot site with real chargers and real drivers.
