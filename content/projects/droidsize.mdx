Droidsize Technologies is the product studio I run in New Delhi, co-founded with Neeraj Kumar in 2019. The thesis is simple: we build products for the recurring problems we meet while operating software companies, refine the shared rails behind them, and offer the same practice to a small number of partner teams.

## The products

- **Domain Collective** — one operational workspace for domains, DNS, and renewals across registrars. Live at [collective.domains](https://collective.domains).
- **Sparkles** — an AI-native reading library that turns saved articles into short visual stories. In active development at [trysparkles.app](https://trysparkles.app).
- **TripleWave** — AI drafts professional documents straight onto your letterhead. In active development at [triplewave.co](https://triplewave.co).
- **Cadenza** — a dataset and a brand template become an editable, exportable deck.
- **Merry Magic Mail** — a letter to Santa that answers back. Live every December at [merrymagicmail.com](https://merrymagicmail.com).
- **CopyCanvas** — review interface copy in the context of real screens and flows.
- **RepoPress & Turbocamp** — open-source: a Git-native CMS and a SaaS starter monorepo.

## The operating system

Every product runs on the same research, design, engineering, AI, and release rails. Each product strengthens the system behind the next — that compounding is the studio's real asset, and it's what partner teams get access to.

In August 2026 we relaunched [droidsize.com](https://droidsize.com) — designed and built end-to-end with AI agents in the loop, from information architecture to the animated SVG cover system.
