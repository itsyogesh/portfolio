Coinbox is a desktop crypto portfolio tracker that takes "not your keys, not your coins" seriously about *data* too: everything lives in an encrypted SQLite database on your own machine. No server, no account, no telemetry.

Built with Tauri 2 and Rust, with the Bitcoin Development Kit underneath and hardware-wallet support in the plan. Developed in the open — the issue tracker is the roadmap.
