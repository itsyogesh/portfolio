Wisesama is a defense layer against scams, frauds, and malicious actors in the Polkadot ecosystem — report something suspicious, check an address or a project before you trust it.

It won the **AI × Web3 Synergy track at the Polkadot Prodigy hackathon ($6,000)**, and it isn't standing alone: Relaycode integrates the Wisesama API, so the two tools form the beginnings of a safety layer for the ecosystem. The plan is a public launch followed by a retroactive funding proposal.
