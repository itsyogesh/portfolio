Charge23 Labs is the EV charging company I co-founded with my wife Evisha in August 2023. It's a DPIIT-recognised startup, backed by the Startup India Seed Fund (SISFS), incubated at AIC Sangam — and the most hardware-adjacent thing I've ever worked on.

## What we're building

- **[Chargespot](https://chargespot.app)** — the consumer EV companion: vehicle setup, battery-aware trip planning, charging discovery, session start and monitoring, with OBD telemetry underneath. Behind it sits the operator side — a charging station management system speaking **OCPP and OCPI (v2.0–2.2.1)**, so any compliant charger can join the network.
- **ChargeID** — the aggregator platform for Charging Point Operators: one B2B layer for CPOs to plug their stations into the network.
- **ChargeLink** — a digital twin engine for EVs, built from software first principles — vehicles, batteries, and charging behaviour modelled so predictions and diagnostics improve without waiting on hardware.

## The stack

A Flutter app, a Django backend with a Go station server, TypeScript web dashboards, and Terraform-managed AWS infrastructure — around twenty repositories under the charge23 organisation.

## Honest status

Pre-launch. We raised a small round, built a five-person team, and burned most of it learning how hard EV infrastructure really is. We're still here, still building — the next milestone is a pilot site with real chargers and real drivers.
