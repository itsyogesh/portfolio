Unlockd makes Polkadot vesting legible: connect a wallet and see your vesting schedules, claim history, and live valuations across Polkadot and Kusama — instead of decoding chain state by hand.

Built on Dedot (the modern Polkadot SDK) with LunoKit for wallets, and sharpened during the 2026 Polkadot hackathon season alongside Relaycode's EVM work.
