Every screenshot-beautifier makes static images pretty; none of them touch GIFs. Gifshots wraps animated screen recordings in the same treatment — backgrounds, padding, framing — so a product demo looks deliberate instead of raw.

Built because Relaycode needed good-looking demo GIFs, kept because everything I ship needs them.
